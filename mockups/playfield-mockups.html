What changed from the earlier mockup

  • Matches the shipped board. Foundation piles with a reaction row above (players 1 to 3) and below (players 4 to 6), then your hand, then the Players panel. On a phone the turn strip stays below the hand.
  • Removed: the Sort Hand button (the hand is always sorted), the "Need this turn" pill, the Undo slot banner and the per-pile text tags.
  • Contrast: slot numbers are solid ink, 11px bold or larger. Your own slot is a solid ring on white instead of a coloured number (was 1.5 to 2.3:1). Turn dots and the "You're next" label are readable too (were about 1.4 to 2:1).
  • Reactions: tap your own circle on a pile to cycle 👍, ❤️, 🔥, none. The number and the emoji share one circle.
  • Gameplay cues (new): the foundation pill shows cards left to play and fills as the team plays the deck down; the active player sees "1 of 2 played" beside Your Hand; a pile that would take the selected card as a backward 10 gets a gold ring and a "−10" or "+10" badge; a reaction circle gets a ring in that player's colour (hover for the name); and the tab title becomes "(Your turn) UpNDown".
  • Modern web: visible focus ring and press feedback, reduced-motion-safe pulse, light and dark themes, dvh for the dialog, and a native <dialog> for Settings (open it from the Settings button).

What is wrong today (checked on the shipped screen)

  1. The number fields cannot be edited normally. Every keystroke is clamped, and an empty field snaps back to the old value. In a browser test, Backspace on 99 gave 9, a second Backspace still gave 9, and typing 4 gave 94. You cannot type 45.
  2. On a phone, Save is off screen. At 390x844 the dialog holds 969px of content in 812px and has no fixed footer, so Save and Close sit below the scroll.
  3. The help text is hidden. It lives in title tooltips on an "i" that is not focusable (tabindex="-1", no role). Touch and keyboard users never see the most useful text on the screen.
  4. One flat list of 11 unrelated fields. Deck rules, lobby options, a display option, a cheat and a personal sound preference are mixed together, and on a phone the "i" drops onto its own line.
  5. Irrelevant fields are shown disabled. Solitaire disables 5 of them and adds a note to explain. A guest or mid-game viewer sees a form of greyed-out inputs.
  6. No feedback. The deck size is never shown, a custom combination highlights no preset, and the only validation is one error line at the bottom.

What this version does

  • Grouped by purpose: Difficulty, Cards, Players, what everyone sees, and this device. Cheats are folded away.
  • Presets first, with a live summary and a Custom state. The summary shows the deck size (98 cards) that was never visible before.
  • Right control for each value: the 5 hand sizes and 3 turn minimums are segmented buttons with every option visible. The top card is a slider. Players use native selects. Lowest card moves to Advanced.
  • Help is always visible as one line under each control, with the direction of difficulty (bigger hand is easier).
  • Sheet layout: fixed header and footer, only the middle scrolls, and it is a bottom sheet on phones. Save is disabled until something changes and the Close button becomes Discard.
  • Read-only view is a plain summary list. Solitaire hides what does not apply instead of disabling it.
  • Number field fixed: the Advanced field lets you clear and retype, and shows an inline error instead of snapping back.

Review of this design

  • Trade-off: the lowest card value is one tap deeper. The presets never change it from 2, so I judged that fine, but a host who tunes it often will notice.
  • Decided: no "needed to start" setting. The host starts whenever they want. The server still stores a minimum, so the client would send it fixed at 2 (the server rejects anything below 2), and nothing on the server or in the schema has to change.
  • Sound effects apply immediately and are not part of Save, because they are stored on the device. The "This device" label says so.
  • Known gap: Esc, tapping outside or Discard throws away unsaved edits without asking, as today. A discard confirmation is a follow-up.
  • Code impact: a rewrite of SettingsDialog (about 250 lines), best moved into its own file so App.tsx does not grow. Props, schema and server stay the same. If you do not want the redesign, the typing bug can be fixed on its own in a few lines.
  • Polish left: on a wide screen the right column is half empty and the sheet still scrolls a little (782px of content in 741px). Sound and Cheats could move up beside Players.
  • Not tested: Safari, and real screen readers. The controls are native inputs, so I expect them to work.
