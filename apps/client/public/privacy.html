Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Summary

UpNDown is a card game. You can play as a guest with no account. If you choose, you can sign in with Google so the game remembers your settings and your results. We do not sell your data, show ads or use analytics trackers.

Playing as a guest

  • You pick a player name. Other players in your game see it.
  • Your game is kept while it runs, as described under Games in progress below.
  • Your browser stores your settings on your own device (local storage). While you are in a multiplayer game that is being played, it also stores the game code and that seat's rejoin token (one entry per game, so several tabs can each have their own), so that if you close the page by mistake the home page can offer to put you back in your seat. It is removed when the game ends or you are removed from it, and it is not used for anything else. We do not receive them as an account.
  • Solitaire works without a connection. If you leave a solitaire game unfinished, a saved copy stays in your own browser so you can pick it up later. You can have several unfinished games saved at once; they are kept for up to 30 days. As a guest, solitaire never sends anything to us, and the saved game does not follow you to another device or browser.
  • Guests have no profile and no history.

Games in progress

While a game is running we keep its state, so it can carry on if someone's connection drops and they come back. That state is: the players' in-game names, the cards in each hand, the draw pile and the foundation piles, the game settings, whose turn it is, and a private rejoin token for each seat. For a signed-in player we also note which account holds the seat, so the result can be saved to that account.

  • Today this state is held in the game server's memory only, so it is lost if the server restarts, and it is deleted no later than 30 minutes after the last player was connected.
  • We are adding the option to save it in our database so a game can survive a server restart. When that is switched on, the state above (including the cards in each hand) is kept in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Railway only while the game is in progress. It is deleted when the game is over and the host returns to the lobby, when everyone leaves, and in any case 24 hours after the last player was connected. A seat's private rejoin token is stored only as a one-way hash.
  • We delete a game's state when the game is over and the host returns to the lobby or everyone leaves, and when nobody has been connected to it for a while (the limit above).
  • We do not keep a move-by-move record of games. What remains afterwards is only the summary that a signed-in player gets in their own history, described below.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. We ask Google only for your basic profile (the openid and profile scopes). We do not request your email address, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.

We store:

  • Your Google account identifier, so we can recognize you next time. It is not shown to anyone.
  • The player name you confirm for use in games, and your settings (for example sounds and game options).
  • A record of each game you finish while signed in, or leave early (as the host who ends a multiplayer game, or when you are removed from a game in progress because you were disconnected): result, date, duration, number of players, and your own card counts. For solitaire, this is the only thing sent to us, and only when you finish a game while signed in; a solitaire game you end early is not recorded. We do not store the names or identities of the other players.
  • How long we keep it: your profile, settings and game history stay until you ask us to delete them (see below). A login session lasts up to a year, and ends sooner if you sign out.
  • Which game you are seated in while it is being played, so that if you are signed in you can take your seat back from another device. This is the account note mentioned under Games in progress, and it goes when the game's state is deleted.
  • A login session. Only a hash of the session token is stored on our server, and your browser keeps a cookie that is HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite.

Your statistics are private to you. Other players only ever see the player name you use in a game.

Server logs

Our own application logs do not record your IP address, cookies, session tokens or Google identifiers. Your IP address is used briefly, in memory only, to limit how fast one connection can make requests, and is not stored. Our hosting and network providers (Railway and Cloudflare) may record technical request details such as IP address, time and page requested for their own security and operations, under their own policies.

Where data is kept

The game runs on Railway. Account data, and in future the state of games in progress, is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Railway. Google processes your sign-in under its own privacy policy.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Sign out at any time from the game menu. This ends your session on that device.
  • To delete your account, email privacy@corless.com from any address and say you want your account deleted. We will delete your profile, settings, sessions and all game history within 30 days. Deletion cannot be undone.
  • You can also remove UpNDown's access in your Google Account settings under security, third-party access.

Children

UpNDown is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Questions: privacy@corless.com.
